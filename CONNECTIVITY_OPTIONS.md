# Hisense AC Control - Connectivity Options

## Option 1: WiFi Control (Recommended if your AC has WiFi)

### Does Your AC Have WiFi?

Look for:
- **WiFi icon on the indoor unit** - small antenna symbol or WiFi indicator
- **Lights/indicators** - blinking WiFi light means it's connected
- **Settings menu** - if AC has a built-in display/menu with WiFi option

**If YES - WiFi Enabled:**
1. Your AC likely has a built-in WiFi module
2. App will automatically discover it
3. Control from anywhere on your network
4. No additional hardware needed

**If NO - No WiFi:**
Proceed to Option 2 (IR Control)

### Using the App with WiFi

1. Make sure AC and iPhone are on **same WiFi network** (2.4GHz)
2. Open the app
3. Tap "Scan Again" 
4. App will find your Hisense AC automatically
5. Tap device and control it

## Option 2: IR Control (If AC has no WiFi)

### The Problem
**iPhones don't have infrared (IR) transmitters built-in.** Unlike your AC remote, they can't shoot IR signals.

### The Solution: WiFi IR Blaster

You need a smart IR device that:
- Connects to WiFi
- Has IR transmitter
- Can relay commands from phone → IR → AC

**Popular Options:**

### 1. **Broadlink RM4** (Best Option)
- ~$40-60 USD
- WiFi connected IR blaster
- Works with any IR remote
- App learns your AC remote codes
- Compatible with smart home ecosystems

**Setup:**
1. Buy Broadlink RM4
2. Plug in and connect to WiFi
3. Open Broadlink app
4. "Learn" your AC remote buttons
5. We can update this app to control via Broadlink

### 2. **Tuya Smart IR Hub**
- ~$20-40 USD
- Similar to Broadlink
- WiFi IR blaster
- Cheaper than Broadlink

### 3. **Apple TV (if you have one)**
- Some models have built-in IR
- Can integrate with HomeKit
- More complex setup

### 4. **Google Home Mini/Nest**
- Doesn't have IR transmitter
- But can work with Broadlink/Tuya devices

## Quick Check: Does Your AC Have WiFi?

### Method 1: Check the Unit
Look at your indoor unit for:
- ✓ WiFi icon or antenna
- ✓ Blinking lights (power, WiFi, mode)
- ✓ Model number that includes "WiFi" or "Smart"

### Method 2: Check the Remote
Look at your remote for:
- ✓ "WiFi" button
- ✓ "Smart" indicator
- ✓ QR code (usually means WiFi capable)

### Method 3: Check Hisense App
- Download "Hisense+" app
- Try to add your AC
- If it finds it = WiFi capable
- If not = No WiFi

### Method 4: Network Scan
When you run this app:
- If it finds your AC = WiFi enabled ✓
- If nothing found = No WiFi ✗
